Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Spinfall is a game you play on your own device. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Spinfall has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Your game progress

Your progress (levels, parts, skins, coins and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game which items you bought so it can unlock them. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, Spinfall does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting information (for example if ads are added), this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us.
